Status values: `draft`, `verified`, `conflicting`, `superseded`, `retired`.

Three columns cannot be filled by a search and must not be left blank: `Population/context and fit`, `Strength/limits`, and `License/reuse` are judgements about whether a source supports the claim it is attached to. When the judgement has not been made, write `unknown — owner to assess` rather than nothing. A blank reads as "assessed and unremarkable"; the explicit form reads as what it is, and survives into the handoff where someone can act on it.
