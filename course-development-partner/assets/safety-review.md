# Safety Review

Use this record whenever students will handle equipment, chemicals, biological material, energized systems, radiation, machinery, vehicles, drones, field sites, or any other physical hazard. Apply `references/stem-authenticity.md`.

This skill may draft, organize, or format safety material. It must not originate the safety basis, and its output is never the authority. Every element below requires verification against an authoritative source and approval by the qualified responsible person before students see it.

An unverified, missing, or unreviewed element is a **Blocker**. It prevents teaching-ready status regardless of the quality of the rest of the artifact, in every interaction mode including Auto.
